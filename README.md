# AnswerKeys Hub

Built on a chemistry exam night in Semester 2 — because every student in my batch was scrambling through WhatsApp groups and random Drive links trying to find question bank answer keys the night before exams.

Instead of studying chemistry, I spent that night building this. By morning it was live. My entire batch used it for that exam season, and it eventually spread to students across other Kerala colleges.

## The Problem

No organized space existed for VJEC question bank answer keys. Students were wasting exam-night hours searching, not studying.

## What It Does

What started as a simple overnight project to quickly find Drive links has grown into a comprehensive platform. It proved useful enough that students from outside our college began using it and reached out to say thanks.

Today, students can browse semester-wise subjects and access study resources including:

- Answer Keys
- Notes
- Mind Maps
- Audio Podcasts

Resources are organized by subject and module to reduce exam-time searching and make preparation faster.
